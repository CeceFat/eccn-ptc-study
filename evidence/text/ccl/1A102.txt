〔15 CFR Part 774, Supp. No. 1, ECCN 1A102；eCFR 2026-10-29〕

1A102 Resaturated pyrolized carbon-carbon “parts” and “components” designed for rockets, missiles, or unmanned aerial vehicles capable of achieving a “range” equal to or greater than 300 km. (These items are “subject to the ITAR.” See 22 CFR parts 120 through 130.)
