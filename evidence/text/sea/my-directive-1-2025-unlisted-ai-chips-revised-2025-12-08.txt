           DIRECTIVE OF THE STRATEGIC TRADE CONTROLLER NO. 1/2025
               DECLARATION OF UNLISTED ITEMS UNDER SECTION 12 OF
                          THE STRATEGIC TRADE ACT 2010 [ACT 708]

PURPOSE

1. The purpose of this Directive is to notify all exporters including intermediary
         companies, freight forwarders and other relevant stakeholders of the export,
         transit and transhipment control over Advanced Artificial Intelligence (AI) Chips
         that may be used in a restricted activity but are not prescribed as strategic items
         under section 7 of the Strategic Trade Act 2010 [Act 708].

UNLISTED ITEMS (CATCH-ALL CONTROL)

2. In exercising the powers conferred by paragraphs 6(1)(a), (d) and (g) of Act
         708, the Strategic Trade Controller issues the Directive of the Strategic Trade
         Controller No. 1/2025: Declaration of Unlisted Items under Section 12 of
         the Strategic Trade Act 2010 [Act 708] for Advanced AI Chips.

3. Pursuant to subsection 12(1) of Act 708, any person who intends to export,
         tranship or bring in transit the Advanced AI Chips under the category codes and
         parameters as specified in paragraph 6 and Annex I of this Directives shall
         notify the Strategic Trade Secretariat (STS), Ministry of Investment, Trade
         and Industry (MITI), at least 30 days in advance, if:

         (a) he knows that such Advanced AI Chips are intended to be used, wholly or
                in part, for or in connection with a restricted activity; or

         (b) he has reasonable grounds to suspect that such Advanced AI Chips are
                intended or likely to be used, wholly or in part, for or in connection with a
                restricted activity.

4. For the purpose of this Directive, “restricted activity” as defined in section 2 of
Act 708 means —

        (a) any activity that supports the development, production, handling, usage,
                maintenance, storage, inventory or proliferation of any weapon of mass
                destruction and its delivery systems; or

        (b) participation in transactions with persons engaged in such activities.

                                                             1
5. With this imposition, any person who intends to undertake activities of export,
transhipment or bring in transit of such Advanced AI Chips is required to apply for STA
permit after the relevant Authority decided whether to allow those activities to proceed.
The application to be accompanied with re-export license from originating countries
and export control product classification from manufacturer.

UNLISTED ITEMS CATEGORY CODES AND TECHNICAL PARAMETERS
6. This Directive applies specifically to Advanced AI Chips that fall under the
following unlisted items category codes:

         (i) Category 3: 3U090; 3A001.u;
         (ii) Category 4: 4U090; 4A003.u; 4A004.u; 4A005.u
         (iii) Category 5: 5U992; 5A002.u; 5A004.u.
         Note:
         Comprehensive descriptions and parameters of products covered under this
         Directive is as Annex I.
7. Any entity that falsely declares such items or circumvents permit requirements
         may be subjected to enforcement under the Act 708 and other applicable
         domestic laws.

EFFECTIVE DATE
8. This Directive takes effect immediately until further notice.
