Strategic Goods Control List Overview
The Strategic Goods Controls List specifies the list of military and dual-use goods, software, and technologies regulated under the Strategic Goods (Control) Act.

Last updated on 6 October 2026

Download the full version of the infographics [PDF, 2.76 MB] (opens in new tab) to view the linked resources.
Goods and technology that meet the technical specifications described in the Strategic Goods Control List are subject to controls under the Strategic Goods (Control) Act (SGCA).
The Strategic Goods Control List is found in The Schedule to the Strategic Goods (Control) Order 2026 (opens in new tab) . It includes items controlled by four Multilateral Export Control Regimes:

- Australia Group

- Missile Technology Control Regime

- Nuclear Suppliers Group

- Wassenaar Arrangement
