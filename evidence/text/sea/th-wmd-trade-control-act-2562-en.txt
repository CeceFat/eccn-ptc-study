        CONTROL OF ITEM IN RELATION TO THE PROLIFERATION OF WEAPONS OF MASS DESTRUCTION ACT

                                                              B.E. 2562 (2019)
                                                       _______________________

                    HIS MAJESTY KING MAHA VAJIRALONGKORN BODINDRADEBAYAVARANGKUN
                                      Given on the 26th Day of April B.E. 2562 (2019)
                                           Being the 4th Year of the Present Reign

             His Majesty King Maha Vajiralongkorn Bodindradebayavarangkun is graciously pleased to proclaim
   that:

             Whereas it is expedient to have the Act on the control of Item in Relation to the Proliferation of
   Weapons of Mass Destruction,

             This Act contains provisions in relation to limitation of rights and liberties of the people which
   section 26 in conjunction with section 33, section 37 and section 40 of the Constitution of the Kingdom
   of Thailand so permitted to be carried out by virtue of law.

             Reasons and necessities for the limitation of rights and liberties under this Act are for the nation
   to have effective law on the control of Item in Relation to the Proliferation of Weapons of Mass
   Destruction, so the security of the nation henceforth established. The Enactment of this law, thus
   conforms to the conditions set forth in section 26 of the Constitution of the Kingdom of Thailand.

             Be it, therefore, enacted by the King by and with the advice and consent of the National
   Legislative Assembly acting as the National Assembly as follows;

             Section 1: This Act shall be called the “Control of Item in Relation to the Proliferation of
   Weapons of Mass Destruction Act B.E. 2562”.

             Section 2: This Act shall come into force as from the first day of January B.E. 2563 (2020) except
   for Section 3, Section 4, Section 5, Section 6, Section 7, Section 8, Section 9, Section 10 and Section 11
   that shall come into force from the day of its publication in the Government Gazette.

             Section 3: Exceptions for application of any provisions of this Act to Regulated Activity, to
   academic institutions under the National Education Act, to higher education institutions under the laws
   thereof or to state agencies, shall be enacted by a Royal Decree in accordance with the proposal of the
   Committee. Rules and conditions may be set forth for the application of such exceptions.

             Rules and conditions set forth under paragraph one, shall be prescribed by the ministerial
   regulation.

             Section 4: In this Act,

             “Weapons of Mass Destruction” means nuclear weapon, biological weapon, chemical weapon or
   any other weapons that are with potential to cause harm to a large number of human lives, animals or
   plants, or to cause grave damage to the environment in the manner similar to that of nuclear weapon,
   biological weapon and chemical weapon and shall include their means of delivery, components or
   equipment;

             “Item” means tangible and intangible items, with or without commercial usefulness, including
   containers and packaging thereof and shall include technology and software;

             “Dual-use Item” means Item that could be utilized both civilly and militarily, and could be used
   to design, develop, manufacture, apply, modify, store, or transport Weapons of Mass Destruction or
   could be treated by any means in order to acquire Weapons of Mass Destruction;

             "Item in Relation to the Proliferation of Weapons of Mass Destruction” means Weapons of Mass
   Destruction, armament, and Dual-use Item;

             “Regulated Activity” means export, re-export, transshipment, transit, brokering and shall include
   any activity performed with intention to proliferate Item(s) that are related to Weapons of Mass
   Destruction. In case where Regulated Activity shall include importation, the Royal Decree shall be
   legislated;

             “Brokering” means negotiation, management or agreement resulting in transactions of buying,
   selling or providing Item in Relation to the Proliferation of Weapons of Mass Destruction located outside
   of the Kingdom that leads to the movement of such Item from one country to another;

             “Committee” means the Control of Item in Relation to The Proliferation of Weapons of Mass
   Destruction Committee;

             "Competent Official” means civil servant or government official, holding a position not lower
   than Practitioner Level or equivalent, appointed by the Minister for the execution of this Act;

             “Director-General” means The Director-General of Department of Foreign Trade;
             “Minister” means the minister having charge and control of the execution of this Act;

             Section 5: The Minister of Commerce shall have charge and control of the execution of this Act
   and shall have the power to appoint and remove competent official, issue ministerial regulation, reduce
   or grant exemption from fees for not more than the rates provided at the end of this Act and issue
   notifications for the execution of this Act.

             The ministerial regulation under paragraph one may prescribe rate of the fees differently
   according to the category or technical specification of the Item.

             Such ministerial regulation and notification shall come into force upon their publication in the
   Government Gazette.

                                                             CHAPTER I

    THE CONTROL OF ITEM IN RELATION TO THE PROLIFERATION OF WEAPONS OF MASS DESTRUCTION COMMITTEE

                                                    ____________________

             Section 6: There shall be a committee called “The Control of Item in Relation to The Proliferation
   of Weapons of Mass Destruction committee ” consisting of the Minister of Commerce as Chairperson;
   the Permanent Secretary of the Ministry of Commerce as Deputy Chairperson, the Secretary-General
   of the Anti-Money Laundering Office, the Secretary-General of the National Security Council, the Director
   of the National Intelligence Agency, the Director-General of the Department of Industrial Works,
   the Director-General of the Department of Agriculture, the Director-General of the Department of
   Medical Science, the Director-General of the Customs Department, the Director-General of the Department
   of International Organizations, the Secretary-General of the Food and Drugs Administration,
   the Secretary-General of the Office of Atoms for Peace, the Director of the Defense Industrial Department
   and the Secretary-General of the National Broadcasting and Telecommunication Commissions as members.

             The Director-General shall be member and secretary and shall appoint official of the
   Department of Foreign Trade not exceeding two persons to be the assistant secretary(-ies).

             For the benefit of proceeding according to the duties and powers of the Committee, the
   Committee may resolve to occasionally invite person(s) with knowledge, expertise, or experiences on
   the subject to be considered to the meeting as member(s). In such case, the person so invited who
   attends the meeting shall be considered a member for the meeting such person thereto invited.

             Section 7: The Committee shall have the duties and powers as follows;

             (1) To propose to the Cabinet for their approval of policies and plans for the control of Item in
   Relation to the Proliferation of Weapons of Mass Destruction;

             (2) To suggest measures and guidelines to the Cabinet for the purpose of prescribing measures
   under Section 12;

             (3) To supervise and provide advice on the operations taken by government agencies and
   private sectors on the control of Item in Relation to the Proliferation of Weapons of Mass Destruction;

             (4) To appoint a sub-committee to provide advice regarding Item in Relation to the Proliferation
   of Weapons of Mass Destruction and other operations as assigned by the Committee;

             (5) To exercise any other missions prescribed by law to be duties and powers of the Committee.

             The measures or guidelines under (2) and the supervision under (3) shall be under the responsibility
   of certain authorities and shall not cause any unnecessary burden on the people.

             Rules and procedures for the appointment of the sub-committee(s) under (4) shall be in
   accordance with the notification issued by the Minister. The appointment of such sub-committee shall
   be granted only as necessary with specified duration of operation time.

             Section 8: In the execution of this Act, The Director-General may request suggestion from the
   sub-committee with regard to Item in Relation to the Proliferation of Weapons of Mass Destruction and
   technical-risk assessment of such Item. The Director-General shall subsequently report on the suggestion
   to the Committee.

             Section 9: At a meeting of the Committee, the presence of not less than one-half of members is
   required to constitute a quorum.

             At a meeting of the Committee, in a case where the Chairperson does not attend the meeting or
   is unable to perform the duties, the Deputy Chairperson shall preside over the meeting. If both the
   Chairperson and the Deputy Chairperson are unable to attend the meeting or to perform their duties,
   the members present shall elect one among themselves to preside over the meeting.

             A decision of the meeting shall be made by a majority of votes. In casting a vote, each member
   shall have one vote. In case of an equality of votes, the presiding Chairperson shall give one more vote
   to become the ruling vote.

             Section 10: The provision of Section 9 shall be applied to the sub-committee(s) meeting mutatis
   mutandis.

             Section 11: The Department of Foreign Trade shall be responsible for the administrative works
   of the Committee and sub-committee(s) as well as the coordination and the cooperation with
   educational institutions, higher education institutions, government agencies, international organizations, or
   relevant private organizations for the benefit of regulating Item in Relation to the Proliferation of Weapons of
   Mass Destruction.

                                                            CHAPTER II

         CONTROL OF ITEM IN RELATION TO THE PROLIFERATION OF WEAPONS OF MASS DESTRUCTION

                                                _________________________

             Section 12: The Minister, with the suggestion of the Committee, shall have duties and powers to
   specify measures as follows;

             (1) To specify Item to be requiring a licensing or to be requiring a certification certifying
   unrelatedness to the proliferation of Weapons of Mass Destruction,

             (2) To specify Regulated Activity for Item under (1),
             (3) To prescribe any other measures in order to control the proliferation of Weapons of Mass
   Destruction including measures with regard to Item with reasonable doubt concerning a connection
   between an end-use or an end-user and the proliferation of Weapons of Mass Destruction.

             Upon a notification of the measures prescribed under paragraph one, a report shall be made to
   the Cabinet for acknowledgement and shall also be disseminated to the public through the information
   technology system of the Department of Foreign Trade.

             Section 13: After a notification specifying Regulated Activity for the Item requiring the license
   has been issued, no one shall conduct such Regulated Activity unless the license has been obtained from
   the Director-General or a person thereby entrusted.

             The application for the licenses, types of the licenses, issuance of the licenses, forms of the
   licenses, validity of the licenses, renewal of the licenses and the issuance of a license substitute
   document shall be in accordance with the rules, procedures, and conditions prescribed and notified by
   the Minister. The prescription may include the inspection of an internal compliance program, however,
   duration of time for each procedure shall be clearly specified.

             Section 14: A license applicant under section 13 shall be a person having been registered with
   the Department of Foreign Trade according to the rules set forth by the Director-General and shall not
   be with the following prohibited characteristics;

             (1) Obtaining a license which is being suspended or a certification which is being withheld
   according to this Act;

             (2) Obtained a license that had been suspended or a certification that had been withheld
   according to this Act unless the applicant have completely complied with the conditions prescribed;

             (3) Obtained a license that had been revoked according to this Act, unless the revocation occurs
   more than one year before the day of the application submission;

             (4) Having been sentenced by a final judgement to imprisonment for an offence committed
   under this Act.

             In case an applicant is a juristic person, a manager or a representative of the juristic person shall
   be qualified and shall not be with the prohibited characteristics under paragraph one.

             Section 15: In a case where a person conducting Regulated Activity fails to comply with the
   conditions set forth in a license, the Director–General shall issue an order to suspend the license for a
   period not exceeding one hundred and twenty days each time.

             In a case where a person conducting the Regulated Activity fails to rectify the incorrectness or to
   comply with the conditions within the period of time specified under paragraph one, the Director-General shall
   issue an order to revoke the license.

             Section 16: After a notification specifying Regulated Activity for the Item requiring the
   certification has been issued, no one shall conduct such Regulated Activity unless the Item have been
   certified.

             The certification, forms of certification and validity of the certification shall be in accordance
   with the rules, procedures, and conditions prescribed and notified by the Minister.

             The provision of section 14 shall apply to the certifier mutatis mutandis.

             Section 17: In a case where a certifier fails to comply with the rules and procedures prescribed
   and notified by the Minister pursuant to section 16 paragraph two, the Director-General shall issue an
   order requesting the certifier to rectify the incorrectness within a specified period of time not exceeding

                                                            CHAPTER III

                                                              APPEALS

                                                   _____________________

             Section 21: Any person exercising Regulated Activity, who does not concur with the order issued
   by the Director-General or a person entrusted under Chapter 2, is entitled to the right to appeal in
   written form to the Minister within thirty days from the date of receipt of the order.

             The appeal lodged under paragraph one shall not stay the enforcement of the order unless the
   Minister orders the stay of the enforcement.

             The decision of the Minister shall be final.

             Section 22: The Minister shall complete the consideration of an appeal within thirty days from
   the date of receipt of such appeal.

             In a case where there exists necessity rendering the consideration unable to be completed
   within the period stated under paragraph one, a written notification shall be made to the appellant
   before the expiration of such period. For this purpose, the period shall be extended not more than two
   times, each time for a period not exceeding thirty days as from the expiration of such period. In the
   event that the consideration remains incomplete, it shall be deemed that the Minister concurs with the
   appellant.

                                                            CHAPTER IV

                                                      COMPETENT OFFICIAL

                                                _________________________

             Section 23: For the execution of this Act, the Competent Official shall have the following power;

             (1) To enter any business establishment or premises in connection with Regulated Activity
   during the period between sunrise and sunset or during office hours of such establishment or premises
   or enter vehicle loaded, or is with probable cause to believe that loaded, with Item in connection with
   the commission of the offence(s) under this Act in order to search or inspect.

             (2) To retain a vehicle loaded or is with probable cause to believe that loaded with Item in
   connection with commission of the offenses under this Act or to request any vehicle, while is within the
   Kingdom, to return to wharf the vessel or to dock at a designated place or port for the purposes of
   inspection and control as per the intention of this Act.

             (3) To seize or sequestrate properties in connection with commission of the offenses under this
   Act for the benefit of legal proceedings, provided that a probable cause to believe that offense(s) under
   this Act is committed exists.

             (4) To issue a summon to person(s) concerned requesting them to give oral statement or to
   furnish documents and evidence necessary for the consideration of Competent Official.

             In exercising the power pursuant to paragraph one (1) and (3), Competent Official shall comply
   with the Criminal Procedure Code, ensure the person(s) concerned prior to the search that he shall fairly
   perform his duty, present the credentials and documents granting power to search to the person
   possessing the dwelling place or premises unless such dwelling place or premises is not being possessed.
   The search shall be conducted in the presence of at least two other individuals. The Competent Official
   shall handover a copy of the documents to the possessor premises as immediately as practical.

             Once entering and conducting the search, if the search processed is not yet completed, it may
   be progressed in nighttime or after the working hours of such establishment or premises.

             Where there is utmost exigency, the search may be operated in nighttime with Competent
   Official with the position category of Professional Level or higher acting as chief of the operation.

             The exercising of power of Competent Official shall be in accordance with the rules, procedures,
   and conditions set forth and notified by the Director-General.

             Section 24: In performing duties pursuant to section 23, person(s) concerned shall provide
   appropriate facilitation.

             Section 25: In performing duties under this Act, Competent Official shall present the Competent
   Official identity card to the person(s) concerned.

             The Competent Official identity card shall conform to the form specified and notified by the
   Director-General.

                                                            CHAPTER V
                                                          CIVIL LIABILITY
                                                   _____________________

             Section 27: Any person conducting Regulated Activity of Item in Relation to the Proliferation of
   Weapons of Mass Destruction in violation of or without complying to this Act, knowing that the end user
   is going to proceed illegally with the Item so as to cause harm to life or body of other persons, the
   person conducting such Regulated Activity shall be collectively liable for the compensation for the
   damage incurred.

                                                            CHAPTER VI
                                                       PENAL PROVISIONS
                                                   _____________________

             Section 28: Any person violating section 13 paragraph one or not complying to the measures
   prescribed pursuant to section 18 shall be liable to a term of imprisonment not exceeding two years or
   to a fine not exceeding two hundred thousand Baht or to both.

             In the event that the violation under paragraph one is committed with an intent to proceed with
   Item in Relation to the Proliferation of Weapons of Mass Destruction to cause harm to other persons, or
   to proceed with such Item to design, develop, manufacture, operate, modify, store, or transport
   Weapons of Mass Destruction, or to proceed by any means with such Item in order to acquire Weapons
   of Mass Destruction, the violator shall be liable to a term of imprisonment not exceeding ten years or to
   a fine not exceeding one million Baht or to both.

             Item involved in the perpetration of the offense under paragraph two shall be forfeited.
             Section 29: Any person providing false information or concealing facts that should be revealed
   to the Competent Official with an intent to acquire a license to conduct Regulated Activity under section
   13 paragraph one, or with an intent to be qualified under the measures prescribed under section 18,
   such person shall be liable to a term of imprisonment not exceeding one year or to a fine not exceeding
   one hundred thousand Baht or to both.
             Section 30: Any person perpetrating offenses under section 28 or section 29 outside the
   Kingdom of Thailand shall be punished in the Kingdom according to this Act, provided that the case

   NOTE: The reasons for the enactment of this Act is due to the United Nations Security Council issuance
   of the resolution of the United Nations Security Council No. 1540 (2004) on April 28, B.E. 2547 (2004)
   and other resolutions related thereto, requesting member states to establish domestic measures to
   prevent the proliferation of weapons of mass destruction that are with potential to cause harm to a
   large number of human lives, animals or plants, or to cause grave damage to the environment including
   general goods that could be assembled, developed, enhanced and or treated by any other means in
   connection with the weapons of mass destruction, by establishing a control system for the exportation,
   the re-exportation, the transshipment, the transit, the brokering and any activity performed with
   intention to proliferate items that are related to the weapons of mass destruction, and to establish
   measures relating to items with reasonable doubt with regard to the relation between an end-use or an
   end-user of such items and the proliferation of the weapons of mass destruction whereby Thailand, as a
   member state of the United Nations is obliged by Article 25 of the Charter of the United Nations to
   comply therewith. Nevertheless, the laws governing the matters at the present time are inadequate for
   a complete implementation of such resolutions, particularly on the control of brokering and intangible
   goods. Therefore, it is a necessity to establish a committee - based system for the policy and guideline
   planning for the control of the proliferation of the weapons of mass destruction in order to ensure
   comprehensive integration of operations among agencies concerned, to establish a license - based system,
   or any other measures for the activities regulated and items in relation to the proliferation of the
   weapons of mass destruction to be prescribed to ensure national security and public safety, and also to
   prescribe appropriate civil liabilities, administrative and penal penalties so that Thailand could control
   the proliferation of the weapons of mass destruction effectively. In that respect, it is necessary for this
   Act to be enacted.
